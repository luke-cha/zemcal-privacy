A little more room for the week.

Open ZemCal from the menu bar for a month grid and a compact, tree-style agenda. Choose which macOS calendars to show, pin the calendar open, or let it disappear when you click away.

Month and agenda together

Move between months, jump to today, and scan the next seven days without opening a full calendar window.

Choose what you see

Select calendars and adjust reminder behavior for each calendar from one place.

Reminders that stay in your hands.

Choose whether to be reminded about supported online meetings or all timed events. Set the timing and display style, then join, snooze, or dismiss. ZemCal never joins a meeting automatically.

Notice the moment

Use a full-screen reminder or a small panel, with an optional gentle chime.

Make it yours

Choose White, Beige, or Black, create a custom palette, and adjust text size.

Private by design.

ZemCal reads calendars on your Mac through Apple EventKit after you grant access. Calendar details and preferences stay on your device. No account, cloud sync, analytics, or tracking.
